# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss evidence, code, and ideas without
personal attacks. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Give
specific, constructive feedback and respect privacy and confidentiality.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, doxxing, and repeated
disruptive conduct are not acceptable in project spaces. Do not publish
someone's private information in issues, discussions, or example datasets.

Maintainers may remove harmful material, warn participants, restrict participation,
or exclude repeat offenders. Responses should be proportional and consistent.
Report concerns privately to the maintainer through a contact channel listed
on the `lordarcane666` GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, open
a minimal issue asking for one without identifying affected people or sharing
sensitive details. No response-time guarantee is promised.
